The avionics lead shall be accountable for the creation and safe testing of the avionics system of the Argonia Cup rocket. The accountable lead shall be sure of the integrity of the avionics system as well as rigorously test the system to validate the system under operating conditions. This lead also holds responsibility with teaching and nurturing the knowledge of their members who desire to learn of this system and holds obligation to consider/address design choices of those who offer it. Shall the accountable lead not hold to the values of safety and learning their position can be subject to evaluation. 

The avionics lead shall also ensure all electronics,  batteries, mounting, and materials are compliant with the Argonia Cup challenge as this is crucial to the teams ability to compete.
